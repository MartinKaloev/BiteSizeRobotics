🧠 ATTENTION-BASED DRL

Attention-based Deep RL treats an agent's trajectory — states, actions, rewards over time — as a sequence, and uses self-attention (à la Decision Transformer) so the policy can attend over its own past context to decide the next action, instead of relying only on the current state.
